# Snowman's Little Planet

## Register

brand

## Users

The owner and visitors to a personal site. No specific writing topic or audience
has been chosen yet. Do not invent a biography, published posts, or projects.

## Product Purpose

Start with a playful, memorable personal home that can grow into a blog.
The current deliverable is one interactive homepage, published through GitHub
Pages. It does not yet have blog authoring or articles. The user approved the
snowy little planet direction inspired by flo-bit.dev after considering Raycast.

## Brand Personality

Wintry, curious, quietly playful. An inhabited miniature world, not a dashboard
or a commercial product landing page.

## Anti-references

Do not clone the reference site's identity, assets, models, or biography.
Avoid generic feature grids, promotional claims, placeholder articles, and
decoration that competes with the planet.

## Design Principles

- The planet is the primary experience, unframed and full-bleed.
- Snowman is immediately identifiable in the first viewport.
- Let the content grow; empty collections stay hidden.
- Interactions should feel discoverable and remain optional.
- Keep future long-form reading calm and legible.
- Yuanyuan (圆圆) is the owner's golden-shaded cat, born in February 2025.
  The owner selected B, the original furry golden pixel cat, not a photo or 3D cat.
  Its small mint/icy platform is 2.5D, with a visible top and shaded sides.
  The cat looks toward the pointer, then walks on the projected ground plane
  in both lateral and depth directions. Paws stay inside the platform edges.
  Age updates by calendar month in Asia/Shanghai; no birth day is assumed.
  Touch taps and focused arrow keys set a destination; vertical scrolling
  remains available. Reduced motion keeps the scene static.
  No roaming cursor pet outside the section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Builder defaults: keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, named icon
buttons, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and a small-screen layout.
No special accessibility needs have been specified.

## Prototype Scope

One homepage composition, as explicitly agreed with the user. No variant
switcher, fake content, persistence, or CMS. The owner subsequently approved
committing, pushing, and publishing the homepage to the user site's GitHub
Pages address. Deployment builds `dist/` from `main` using GitHub Actions.

## Yuanyuan Style Exploration

The owner requested all four proposed 2D directions as furry comparison samples:
A soft dumpling, B pixel resident, C peeking cat, D storybook cat.
The owner subsequently selected B for the homepage. Preserve the separate
comparison page: its bodies stay fixed and only eyes look toward the mouse.
Use illustrated fur, not realistic rendering or 3D. A sample radio selection
alone does not replace the homepage.
